# Phase 7.2 developmental order and identity results

## Question

Phase 7.2 asks whether developmental history is computationally constitutive,
rather than a decorative log: can two ecologies receiving the same episode
multiset develop different capabilities solely because of order, and do those
capabilities follow checkpoints rather than surface labels? It also weakens the
stable-identity assumption, makes merge choice selective, and expands synthetic
mechanism replication beyond the three Phase 7.1 targets.

## Matched curriculum-order experiment

Three matched pairs receive exactly the same two episodes:

1. an episode from which a reusable local complement macro can be constructed;
2. a target episode requiring complement plus another operation.

The episode-local synthesis budget is four. Without the prerequisite, the
three target functions cost five or seven in the original basis and remain an
explicit unresolved tension. Once complement has been constructed and stored,
calling it reduces the target program to cost four. The learner searches the
bounded language from observed semantics; target expressions are not selected
by a mechanism-name lookup.

The forward curriculum (`prerequisite → target`) acquires the target in all
three pairs. The reverse curriculum (`target → prerequisite`) does not revisit
the earlier unresolved episode and therefore lacks it in all three pairs. This
is a controlled path-dependence result, not an assertion that all useful
development must be order-sensitive.

Renaming/transplanting the developed checkpoint preserves all three
capabilities; resetting to an evidence-matched empty developmental state removes
all three. Capability follows state and history rather than an instance label.

## Uncertain linkage and identity

Across 64 independently seeded episodes, input and consequence fragments have
unrelated local IDs, independently shuffled arrival order, and jittered clocks.
Nearest-time consequence alignment recovers the hidden pairing with accuracy
`1.000000`; illicit positional alignment scores `0.088542`. Permuting owner
labels leaves inferred alignment accuracy at `1.000000`.

When two candidates are exactly equidistant, the linker abstains instead of
inventing a unique identity. The test therefore weakens global case identity to
inferred temporal identity with an explicit uncertainty state. It does not yet
solve semantic record linkage or dense asynchronous event streams.

## Evidence-constrained merge

A transparent merge policy accepts two specialists only if their executable
artifacts are non-conflicting and their union adds capability beyond either
parent. It accepts a complementary pair, rejects a pair with incompatible
semantics for the same operator identity, and preserves both accepted
capabilities through checkpoint/restart.

This replaces unconditional test-driven merge invocation with a bounded
evidence rule. It is not a learned social ontology or a general negotiation
process.

## Mechanism-population stress

The synthesis engine is evaluated on 64 independently sampled three-input
Boolean denotations. Each truth-table consequence is recovered by majority from
11 independently corrupted observations with a 10% flip probability before
program synthesis; private truth is used only for scoring. Accuracy is
`1.000000`; the family-level Wilson 95%
interval is approximately `[0.943374, 1.000000]`.

The family—not a correlated truth-table row—is the uncertainty unit. This is a
real improvement over reporting only three hand-selected targets, but all 64
families still inhabit the same finite Boolean universe and primitive closure.

## Interpretation

The constitutive-history claim survives a direct matched-multiset test: what an
ecology can do depends on what it could already do when a later episode
arrived. That capability follows checkpoint state under renaming and disappears
under reset. Uncertain temporal alignment, explicit abstention, selective merge,
and family-level replication also behave as theorized.

The experiment addresses four earlier boundaries:

* three targets become a 64-family stress population;
* stable global case IDs become inferred temporal linkage;
* identity labels are separated from checkpoint-owned capability;
* merges become evidence-constrained rather than unconditional.

It does not eliminate finite domains, the supplied primitive basis, the supplied
cost constitution, lack of language perception, or lack of external physical
consequences. Those are not all mere data-volume problems: some are deliberate
constitutional choices that must be compared, learned locally, or falsified in
later phases rather than simply scaled away.
