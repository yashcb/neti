# Research reconciliation and decision log

## Precise hypothesis

A persistent heterogeneous ecology can use a sparse, structurally apt cue to
coordinate partial developmental histories and form an unseeded executable
concept. The concept must causally improve behavior on an unseen surface,
survive into a later inquiry, reorganize prior traces, and fail to appear under
a matched irrelevant cue.

Success is conjunctive. Every predicate in `ExperimentReport.criteria` must be
true; no aggregate reward may hide a failed ablation, control, genealogy, or
transfer requirement.

## Relationship to the source documents

The implementation preserves the load-bearing commitments shared by the
project and technical specification:

- persistent developmental state rather than stateless answer generation;
- heterogeneous local traces and vocabularies;
- broadcast resonance rather than a fixed expert router;
- temporary coalitions and typed tensions;
- boundary objects rather than forced representational identity;
- executable concepts whose removal changes behavior;
- event-sourced lineage, revisability, and replay validation;
- multiple aptness dimensions rather than one global score;
- cue-dependence, false-depth, transfer, and persistence controls.

It departs from conventional mixture-of-experts work by making developmental
history and ontology-changing events part of the object under evaluation, not
merely an implementation detail behind a shared objective. It draws on
hypergraph/associative representations, causal and counterfactual modeling,
quality-diversity ecology, boundary objects, autopoiesis, and event sourcing,
but this first experiment implements only the operational intersection needed
to test the minimal hypothesis.

## Reconciliation findings and explicit interpretations

1. **The prose is normative where equations are damaged.** Several displayed
   formulas in `project_technicalities.md` have missing operands. Metric names,
   prose definitions, algorithms, and the decisive success list determine this
   implementation. No guessed scalar formula is introduced.
2. **This phase is a controlled experiment, not the entire architecture.** The
   specification describes open-ended birth, splitting, merging, death, tool
   construction, language development, and environmental intervention without
   enough mechanics to implement them honestly. The event vocabulary is
   extensible, but those claims remain future work.
3. **A concept must be operational.** The emergent handle is only a local name.
   Its scientific content is the fitted lagged, bounded prediction operator;
   crystallization requires multi-domain predictive gain.
4. **The hidden structure cannot be a discovery label.** Domain identifiers and
   the phrase “delayed feedback under capacity constraints” are absent from
   candidate selection. Candidate lags and coefficients are selected using
   pooled normalized observations.
5. **Ablation means replacement by the pre-concept behavior.** Removing the
   operator restores the persistence predictor, so ablated gain is zero by
   definition and the measured treatment-minus-ablation gain is the held-out
   gain.
6. **Persistence is bounded.** The current check invokes the concept from
   instance state during a subsequent held-out inquiry in the same process. It
   does not establish survival across arbitrary future distribution shift or
   indefinite deployment.
7. **Cue understanding is partly architect-supplied.** Local traces acquire a
   small structural vocabulary and resonance uses explicit lexical anchors.
   Operator discovery is data-driven, but open-ended language grounding is not
   claimed.

## Load-bearing assumptions and risks

- Paired driving-signal/state series and physical capacity are observable. If
  capacity were latent, normalization and cross-surface alignment would require
  another learned concept and could materially change the result.
- The three training worlds really share a low-order stationary mechanism. A
  successful transfer does not imply robustness to causal heterogeneity.
- Candidate lag search (one through six) contains the generating lag. This is a
  deliberately constrained hypothesis class and a major source of architectural
  prior.
- Surface diversity is synthetic. Renaming and rescaling prevent direct label
  copying but are much weaker than genuinely different real-world ontologies.
- The cue is sparse relative to the event lineage, but its lexical construction
  is hand-designed. Cue paraphrase robustness is not yet established.
- Predictive gain is evidence of operational reality, not proof of metaphysical
  novelty, consciousness, or general interpretation.
- Instances are heterogeneous in observation history and interpretive bias, but
  share conventional Python machinery. This tests developmental behavior, as
  permitted by the technical specification, not machine-native substrates.

## Running decision log

| Decision | Rationale | Consequence / falsifier |
|---|---|---|
| Use deterministic seeded micro-worlds | Makes treatment, irrelevant-cue control, and ablation exactly reproducible | Results that depend on uncontrolled sampling noise are rejected |
| Normalize only inside a boundary object | Allows shared roles without erasing local surface names | Unknown capacity is outside the present claim |
| Give each instance only one or two worlds | No individual receives the complete cross-domain evidence | Coalition and lineage remain necessary |
| Select coalitions by resonance threshold and retain bias diversity | Avoids a fixed domain router or single winner | Irrelevant cue must not create a coalition/concept |
| Pool sequences only after inquiry formation | Prevents an omniscient learner from bypassing ecology | Without relevant resonance, discovery does not run |
| Search candidate executable operators | Makes conceptual content testable and ablatable | A label without gain cannot crystallize |
| Report criteria and aptness components separately | Avoids mode collapse and metric compensation | `success` is strict conjunction |
| Retain translation residue and full causal ancestry | Makes cross-domain compression inspectable | Missing ancestry fails genealogy validation |

## Next decisive extensions

1. Learn boundary roles and capacity rather than supplying them.
2. Pre-register a larger family of relevant, paraphrased, adversarial, and
   semantically similar but structurally irrelevant cues.
3. Compare ecology against an equal-compute pooled learner and isolated-instance
   baselines to measure ecological synergy rather than mere task success.
4. Add nonstationary worlds where old concepts require revision or splitting.
5. Serialize ecology state, reload it after unrelated inquiries, and measure
   persistence over a pre-registered horizon.
6. Permit generated operators outside the architect-provided lag family and
   penalize hypothesis-class complexity.
