# Phase 1–4 falsifiers and Phase 5–6 protocol

## Why falsification precedes extension

The first four phases depended on exact binary capacity, complete aligned
cases, stable fragment order, one generating mechanism, and operator state
serialized beside rather than inside an interpretive instance. The advanced
programme attacks those assumptions before treating their outputs as the
substrate for active inquiry and ecological revision.

## Phase 1–4 falsifier suite

Thirty-two independently seeded episodes vary lag from one through five and
cycle across `and`, `or`, `xor`, and `xnor` mechanisms. Raw observations are
continuous noisy mixtures around two latent levels with unknown scale. A
deterministic two-means procedure infers both the latent bits and physical
scale. Reports include a mean, a normal 95% diagnostic interval, and transparent
hypothesis-evaluation counts.

Further interventions remove every seventh case, permute fragment storage
order, and checkpoint an executable specification inside its developmental
instance. Missing cases are joined by durable identity rather than compressed
list position. Owner permutation tests storage invariance, not owner anonymity.

The suite remains falsifiable: it fails if scale error exceeds eight percent,
the lower noisy-transfer interval crosses 95%, missing-case or owner-permutation
accuracy falls below 99%, the grammar fails a mechanism family, or checkpoint
restoration loses the executable artifact.

### Remaining boundary

Two-cluster recovery still assumes bimodality; stable owner identity remains
available; and the Boolean grammar remains architect supplied. The reported
normal interval is a repeatability diagnostic, not external replication.

## Phase 5 — active inquiry under observational equivalence

Passive history makes two models exactly equivalent:

1. the outcome follows a driver delayed by two steps;
2. the outcome follows a contemporaneous proxy that happens to equal that
   delayed driver.

More passive observation cannot distinguish them. Their contradictory adequacy
creates a typed tension and a question about which variable survives
intervention. Action selection compares continued observation with randomizing
the driver while holding the former proxy fixed. Only the latter has model
separation, so the inquiry selects it under a cost and reversibility profile.

The consequence revises relative plausibility without assigning probability
one, resolves the tension, installs a causal delay operator inside the instance,
and records the causal lineage. A counterfactual run without intervention must
preserve plurality rather than fabricate resolution.

## Phase 6 — nonstationary revision and ecological specialization

The initial regime is explained perfectly by an unconditional delayed-response
concept. A second regime adds context-dependent XOR behavior. Revision requires
at least three pre-registered 32-step windows above a 25% residual floor, which
prevents a single noisy error from rewriting ontology.

The old concept is not retracted globally. It is marked as overloaded and split
into scoped descendants:

- unconditional delayed response for the original regime;
- conditional delayed response for the context-active regime.

Identity strain simultaneously makes the parent instance dormant and creates
two active specialists. Both inherit the parent's graph, field, models,
language, history, and executable artifacts before specializing. Concept nodes
retain parent lineage, scope edges retain tension lineage, and a complete JSON
checkpoint must preserve both executable descendants.

## Interpretation boundary before Phase 7

Positive results would establish active model discrimination, scoped revision,
lineage-preserving specialization, and executable persistence in controlled
worlds. They would not yet justify unconstrained corpus training. Phase 7 should
begin with an episodic developmental corpus whose records include prior
checkpoint, instance-owned observations, live models, tension, chosen or
rejected intervention, consequence, accepted delta, and later transfer test.

Before scaling that corpus, the next controls should add asynchronous evidence,
continuous non-bimodal latent states, unsafe or irreversible intervention
choices, false regime alarms under correlated noise, merge-after-split tests,
and held-out primitive families not contained in the initial grammar.

These six controls are now executable and reported in
[`phase_7_readiness.md`](phase_7_readiness.md). Their positive predicates permit
a bounded episodic pilot, not unrestricted corpus scaling. In particular, the
held-out NAND control intentionally demonstrates the initial grammar's 0.75
ceiling; an oracle table reaches 1.0 but does not count as autonomous primitive
invention.

## Deterministic reference outcome

Under the committed default configuration, all falsifier, Phase 5, and Phase 6
predicates pass. The important measurements are:

- mean noisy scale error: approximately `0.00417`;
- lower noisy-transfer interval: `1.0` across 32 episodes;
- Phase 5 passive accuracy: `1.0` for both observationally equivalent models;
- Phase 5 interventional separation: approximately `0.516`;
- Phase 5 held-out accuracy after revision: `1.0`;
- Phase 6 old-concept accuracy after regime change: approximately `0.516`;
- Phase 6 scoped child accuracy: `1.0` in both regimes;
- Phase 6 false revision rate in the stationary control: `0.0`.

These exact synthetic outcomes validate implementation invariants. They are not
confidence claims about open-world performance. The CLI exits non-zero if any
predicate fails, so archived runs cannot silently discard a failed dimension.
