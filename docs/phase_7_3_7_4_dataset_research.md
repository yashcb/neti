# Phase 7.3–7.4 dataset research: grounding, language, and philosophical material

## Decision in one sentence

A philosophical and metaphysical corpus is useful as a **linguistic and
conceptual stress layer**, but it is not sufficient as the decisive Phase 7.3
dataset and must not be treated as a store of causal truth. Phase 7.3 first
needs controlled action–consequence records; Phase 7.4 can then combine
versioned human inquiry traces with carefully constructed philosophical
contrast episodes.

## Why this follows from the earlier phases

Phases 1–4 established a deliberately narrow operational notion of a concept:
distributed evidence can identify a mechanism, translation matters, and an
executable operator matters independently of its name. Phases 5–6 added
intervention, warranted revision, scoped plurality, specialization, lineage,
and persistence. Phases 7.0–7.2 made episodes replayable and showed that order
and owned history can change later capability. None of those results licenses
the inference that intelligence is identical to an algorithm plus a dataset.
They establish a method for making some claims about knowing empirically
answerable: a change counts when it reorganizes future possibilities and the
gain disappears under the corresponding causal ablation.

The project should therefore resist two reductions at once:

1. **linguistic reduction:** fluent metaphysical discourse is not evidence that
   the system has formed an operational concept;
2. **behaviorist reduction:** prediction alone does not exhaust knowing, because
   the ecology must also preserve uncertainty, reasons, translation residue,
   counterfactual sensitivity, identity, and the ability to revise its own
   conceptual organization.

Language is not merely decoration here. It can carry distinctions, reasons,
analogies, modal commitments, defeaters, and questions that are not present in
raw sensor streams. But language becomes evidence only through a temporally
lawful episode: what was available to whom, which alternatives were live, what
was asked or done, what later became observable, and which internal change
proved reusable.

## The core distinction: semantic nutrition versus empirical closure

Philosophical material is unusually rich in:

* distinctions whose boundaries are contested rather than given;
* arguments, objections, counterexamples, and conceptual revision;
* modal, normative, first-person, and explanatory vocabulary;
* explicit disagreement about what would count as knowledge or explanation;
* long-range lineage in which a later position inherits and transforms an
  earlier one.

Those properties make it valuable **semantic nutrition** for translation,
plural model retention, concept splitting, analogy, question formation, and
abstention. They do not normally provide interventions, calibrated sensors,
missing counterfactuals, or independently observable closure. A corpus of
finished essays also leaks polished concepts and rewards retrospective
imitation. Thus philosophy should not replace the controlled external system
required by Phase 7.3.

A useful rule is:

> Use controlled worlds to test whether a concept works; use philosophical
> contrasts to test whether the system can articulate, delimit, challenge, and
> revise what the concept could mean.

Neither layer alone is an adequate theory of intelligence.

## Recommended evidence stack

### Tier A — Phase 7.3 physical grounding (confirmatory)

Use the **Causal Chambers** as the leading candidate. The project provides real
physical systems, downloadable datasets, mechanistic models, apparatus
information, and case studies spanning interventions, time series,
out-of-distribution sensors/images, change points, and symbolic regression.
That combination is unusually close to the repository's need for actuator,
sensor, timing, regime, and mechanism strata. The source's own experimental
repository directs users to the chamber site for datasets and construction
resources and publishes reproducibility notebooks under MIT terms
([Causal Chambers project](https://causalchamber.org/),
[official experiment repository](https://github.com/juangamella/causal-chamber-paper),
[paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.11341)).

Before adoption, freeze and audit the **dataset** license separately from the
code license, enumerate independent physical runs (not rows), verify actual
intervention timestamps and apparatus versions, and confirm that at least 80
independent held-out run/block episodes can be formed. If that count is too
small, use the chambers as a high-quality pilot rather than inflating the unit
of analysis.

Two useful secondary strata are:

* the NASA Prognostics Center battery records, which include charge,
  discharge, impedance, temperature, current, voltage, and run-to-failure
  experimental profiles; these are valuable for degradation, nonstationarity,
  and delayed consequences, but preset cycling is not equivalent to an action
  selected online by this ecology
  ([NASA PCoE data repository](https://www.nasa.gov/intelligent-systems-division/discovery-and-systems-health/pcoe/pcoe-data-set-repository/));
* a version-frozen Tennessee Eastman Process simulator for abundant fault and
  control episodes. It is a high-fidelity synthetic/process-control stratum,
  not an independent physical replication, so it must be labelled between the
  current Layer A and Layer B rather than called real-world confirmation
  ([original process-control paper record](https://doi.org/10.1016/0098-1354(93)80018-I)).

The first adapter should expose raw channels and anonymous action identifiers,
not mechanism names. It should assign actuator, state, and consequence
projections to different instances before outcomes are inspected, require a
prospective prediction at the intervention boundary, and retain the isolated,
pooled, linkage, translation, passive-only, operator, and history-reset arms.

### Tier B — Phase 7.4 consequential human inquiry (shadow evaluation)

The strongest first source class is software development because proposed
changes have executable consequences. **SWE-bench** supplies real GitHub issues,
repository snapshots, patches, and a containerized test harness; its Verified
subset contains 500 engineer-screened tasks. Use its collection machinery to
construct new, time-held-out episodes rather than reporting a contaminated
public leaderboard result. Hide the accepted patch and future tests until the
ecology commits diagnoses, questions, and a candidate action
([SWE-bench documentation](https://www.swebench.com/),
[official repository](https://github.com/SWE-bench/SWE-bench)).

SWE-bench is still a task benchmark, not a complete developmental trace. Enrich
newly collected cases with issue chronology, competing attempted patches,
review comments, CI jobs, reverts, and a later regression window. Treat merge
or maintainer acceptance as a social decision; treat tests and later failures
as observable but incomplete consequences.

For scholarly disagreement, use **OpenReview** only as a source of versioned
notes, replies, revisions, and decisions obtained under its current API and
venue policies. A score or acceptance is a social outcome, never a truth label.
The API exposes notes and invitation-governed content, but public visibility
does not by itself settle redistribution, privacy, or per-paper licensing; take
and store a source-specific legal/ethics snapshot
([OpenReview API guide](https://docs.openreview.net/getting-started/using-the-api),
[OpenReview terms](https://openreview.net/legal/terms)).

Wikimedia revision histories and discussion pages can supply negotiated-edit
chains at large volume. Preserve timestamps and parent revisions, pseudonymize
contributors where appropriate, and obey attribution/share-alike requirements.
Final wording and consensus are social closure, while later reverts, citations,
or policy-compliant edits are consequences with limited epistemic meaning
([Wikimedia dumps](https://dumps.wikimedia.org/),
[Wikimedia reuse guidance](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use)).

### Tier C — philosophical/metaphysical contrast corpus (developmental and
adversarial, not confirmatory truth)

Build this corpus rather than expecting one existing dataset to have the needed
shape. Source candidate texts from materials whose licenses permit the intended
processing and redistribution: public-domain works from Project Gutenberg,
open-access works indexed by the Directory of Open Access Books, and
individually verified open manuscripts. Metadata services can aid discovery,
but metadata availability is not a full-text license
([Project Gutenberg permission information](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/permission.html),
[DOAB](https://www.doabooks.org/)).

Do not begin by maximizing token count. Construct **contrast families** around
questions such as identity through change, causation, grounding, modality,
induction, testimony, explanation, realism, normativity, and the relation
between language and world. A family should contain genuinely incompatible
positions, internal variations, objections, replies, and later transformations;
no school should be installed as an answer key.

Each philosophical episode should distinguish:

```text
source passage and date
speaker/work identity and lineage
claims (speaker commitments, not facts)
premises and inferential links
presuppositions and scope
terms plus competing local senses
live objections and defeaters
counterexample or thought-experiment setup
available inquiry moves
chosen historical move
later reply/revision (held out)
external formal/empirical consequence, if any
closure status: observed | social | open | in-principle-underdetermined
```

The ecology should see raw passages and chronology. Argument graphs,
translations, conceptual boundaries, and proposed operators are system outputs.
Curator annotations may be private evaluation data, preferably with multiple
annotators and explicit disagreement. Thought experiments are interventions on
a representation, not interventions on the world; report them as a separate
epistemic type.

## A scalable construction pipeline

### 1. Preregister the ontology of records, not the philosophical answers

Freeze the episode schema, allowed source classes, temporal cutoff, exclusions,
license policy, privacy policy, independent unit, and leakage canaries. Define
closure types before collection. Never use labels such as `correct_position`,
`true_metaphysics`, or mechanism-bearing episode IDs.

### 2. Acquire immutable source snapshots

Store content digests, retrieval dates, source URLs, revision/commit IDs,
license evidence, and deletion obligations. Keep raw restricted material outside
redistributable artifacts. Publish adapters and hashes when the underlying text
cannot be republished.

### 3. Extract candidates with machines; adjudicate with people

Parsers and language models may propose claim spans, relations, term senses,
objections, and chronology links. They must not silently create the gold record.
Two trained annotators should independently adjudicate high-value evaluation
families; a domain editor resolves only where a forced resolution is legitimate,
and otherwise records plurality. Measure agreement per field rather than with
one aggregate number.

### 4. Convert chronology into prospective episodes

Cut every chain at a real decision boundary. Reveal only artifacts available at
that time. Require the ecology to preserve alternatives, ask a question, make a
prediction, propose an intervention, translate a distinction, or abstain. Then
reveal the later artifact and record the state delta. Retrospective summaries
without a held-out future are reading-comprehension examples, not Phase 7
episodes.

### 5. Generate volume by recombination only on development data

Large volume should come from many independent source chains and multiple
lawful cuts through a chain, not paraphrasing one conclusion thousands of
times. Synthetic renderings can vary vocabulary, observation ownership,
arrival order, missingness, and translation interfaces while preserving the
underlying public event graph. Keep all variants from one source family in one
split and count the family once for uncertainty.

### 6. Maintain three partitions and a source registry

Use a smoke/adjudication partition, a developmental curriculum, and a frozen
source-held-out test. Hold out whole authors, works, repositories, apparatus
versions, philosophical question families, and surface renderers. Maintain a
registry recording whether every item is physical consequence, executable
consequence, social consequence, textual reply, or open question.

### 7. Evaluate constitutive change, not answer resemblance

Report whether the internal change improves a later consequence or disciplined
handling of an open question; whether translation, operator, and history
ablations remove the gain; whether confidence and abstention match closure; and
whether minority interpretations survive when evidence does not warrant
collapse. Compare the same encoder in isolated, ecological, and equal-evidence
pooled arms.

## Proposed initial volumes

Keep the existing planning targets and add a bounded philosophical pilot:

| Stratum | Development | Frozen evaluation | Unit |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Causal Chambers / controlled apparatus | 40+ | 80+ (then ~180 across 3 strata) | physical run or intervention block |
| Repository/CI chains | 60 mixed human-development cases | 100 | issue-attempt chain |
| Review/rebuttal | included in the 60 | 100 | paper-round chain |
| Negotiated edit/claim revision | included in the 60 | 100 | dispute-revision chain |
| Philosophical contrast pilot | 120 families | 120 families | question–position–objection–reply family |

For the philosophical pilot, target at least 12 broad question domains, at
least four distinct traditions or methodological styles where available, and
no more than 5% of evaluation families from one author or work. These are
design minima, not a power calculation. Estimate clustering and the minimum
effect from development data, then preregister the final family count.

## What learned language components may do

A frozen or locally trained language encoder is probably necessary for term
sense, argument relation, and cross-vocabulary linkage. This does not make the
project “solely an LLM.” The encoder is a perceptual organ inside an ecology:
its outputs are typed, owned, uncertain observations. It must not see future
closures, update on the frozen test, or optimize the whole ecology toward one
acceptance score.

Compare four perception conditions: transparent lexical/argument heuristics,
a frozen pretrained encoder, a locally trained encoder/translator, and the same
encoder with centralized equal-evidence access. Add surface paraphrase,
quotation-attribution, chronology, and mechanism-name canaries. If an LLM is
used to manufacture training annotations, retain model/version/prompt lineage
and ensure the frozen evaluation is independently human-adjudicated.

## Falsifiers and stop conditions

Stop or downgrade the claim if any of the following occurs:

* the controlled source lacks enough independent runs or auditable
  interventions;
* future text, accepted patches, mechanism names, or curator conclusions leak
  into public observations;
* performance follows author/source style rather than held-out consequences;
* philosophy improves verbal coherence but not calibrated plurality,
  transfer, inquiry choice, or operational ablation;
* the ecology does not exceed its strongest isolated member, or an
  equal-evidence pooled learner explains the full result;
* apparent volume is mostly correlated rows, comments, paraphrases, or repeated
  cuts from a few chains;
* annotators are forced to declare truth where the source supports only social
  closure or principled openness;
* harmful or irreversible inquiry is selected because information gain was
  collapsed into a scalar reward.

## Bottom line

The intuition behind adding philosophy is sound, provided its role is stated
precisely. Metaphysical discourse contains the distinctions through which an
intelligence can question what its operational successes mean, notice that two
formulations organize possibility differently, and avoid confusing a useful
regularity with a completed account of reality. It cannot, by textual volume
alone, supply the resistance of a world.

The strongest Phase 7.3–7.4 design is therefore **triangulated**: physical
intervention supplies resistance and causal closure; executable and historical
human processes supply language, reasons, ambiguity, and delayed consequence;
philosophical contrast families supply deep conceptual variation and explicit
underdetermination. The research result to seek is not a model that recites a
better metaphysics. It is an ecology whose owned history changes what it can
notice, ask, translate, test, preserve, and revise—and whose claimed gains
remain visible under prospective consequences and disappear under causal
ablation.
